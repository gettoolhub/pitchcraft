07 / About Pitchcraft

Clarity, crafted
slide by slide.

Pitchcraft is an independent presentation design studio founded and led by Mohsin Sarwar. We help startups, businesses, educators and ambitious professionals turn complex ideas into clear, persuasive visual stories.

Our mission is simple: make every important idea easier to understand, remember and act on. That means combining thoughtful structure, strong storytelling and purposeful design—not decorating slides for decoration’s sake.

We work with clients worldwide across pitch decks, business presentations, sales materials, company profiles, educational content and presentation redesigns. Every engagement is shaped around the client’s message, audience and goals.
